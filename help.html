Help Center

How do I place an order?

Choose a product, add it to your cart, then continue to checkout and complete the demo payment/order flow.

What payment methods are available?

Cash on Delivery, eSewa, Khalti, Bank Transfer and Card are available as frontend demo options.

How can I track my order?

Open My Orders and choose Track Order to see the order timeline.

Can I cancel an order?

In this demo, order status is stored locally. Admin can update the status from the admin dashboard.
